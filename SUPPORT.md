# Getting help

- **Questions and ideas:** [Discussions](https://github.com/mrbandler/senn/discussions).
- **Bugs:** [open an issue](https://github.com/mrbandler/senn/issues/new/choose) with the
  output of `senn --version`.
- **Security problems:** don't open an issue; see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Documentation:** the [book](https://mrbandler.github.io/senn/).

Senn is maintained by one person in their spare time, so answers can take a few days.
